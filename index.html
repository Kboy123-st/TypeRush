ARCADE GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY

  1. 1
    Type the Center Word

    Type the letters shown on the center keycaps into the input field. Each correct letter pops and turns mint green!

  2. 2
    Build Sparkly Combos

    Every completed word increases your Combo. Reach 5x, 10x, and 20x streaks to unlock special arcade celebrations and up to a 4x Score Multiplier!

  3. 3
    Invite a Friend (1v1 Mode)

    Create a room or share an invite link to race a friend at the same time on the exact same word sequence. Watch their live progress bar and score!

  4. 4
    Beat the Clock

    Keep typing until the countdown timer ends. Mistakes reset your combo streak, so stay accurate — highest score wins!
